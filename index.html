Your entries stay on this device unless you export them.

Backup and restore

Export a JSON backup to Files or OneDrive. Import it on another device to copy your logs.

Photo analysis setup

For AI recognition, enter your private photo-analysis service URL. Never paste an API key here. Without a service, you can still photograph a meal and adjust its details manually.

Install on iPhone

Open your hosted Fairway Fuel link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open the new icon once while online to cache the app for offline use.

About your data

No account, ads, or analytics. Data is stored in your browser using localStorage. Clearing website data or deleting the app may erase it, so export backups regularly. This app does not sync with ChatGPT.
